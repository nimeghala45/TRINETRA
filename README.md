**TRINETRA** is a **multi-layered cognitive vehicle safety system** designed to enhance automotive safety through intelligent monitoring, anomaly detection, and centralized control.

---

##  Overview

Modern vehicles lack **integrated intelligence across safety domains** — driver state, cyber threats, environmental risks, and system failures operate in silos.

**TRINETRA solves this by introducing a centralized safety arbitration engine** that monitors and reacts across multiple risk layers in real-time.

---

##  Problems It Solves

- Driver fatigue and emotional instability
- Vehicle cyber-attacks via CAN bus
- Environmental hazards (e.g., floods)
- Hardware failures (e.g., rodent damage)
- Lack of unified safety response systems

---

##  Core Modules

### 1.  Emotional State-Based Vehicle Arbitration
- Detects driver emotional state
- Restricts unsafe driving behavior

### 2. Fatigue Detection with Active SOS
- Real-time fatigue monitoring
- Automatic emergency alert system

### 3. CAN-Based Cyber Anomaly Detection
- Detects malicious CAN bus activity
- Prevents unauthorized commands

### 4.  Flood-Aware Electrical Isolation
- Detects water exposure
- Isolates critical electrical systems

### 5.  Rodent-Induced Wiring Detection
- Identifies wiring discontinuity
- Prevents electrical hazards

### 6.  Centralized Safety Arbitration Engine
- Aggregates all inputs
- Enforces **PARK mode** under critical risk

---

##  Architecture (Conceptual)
Sensors → Detection Modules → Safety Engine → Vehicle Control (PARK Mode)
To build **next-generation intelligent safety systems** that can become standard in autonomous and semi-autonomous vehicles.
